# Decision: Possible Answers as Embeds

> **Supersedes the Ballots section of
> [Decision 2](2-ballot-and-vote-schema-shape.md)** and reaffirms its Votes
> section.

A ballot stores its possible answers as `embeds_many :possible_answers`
(`Flick.RankedVoting.PossibleAnswer`, with a `value` text field and an
autogenerated id) in a jsonb column, converted in place from the old
comma-separated text column. Comma-separated input broke down for long answers
and could not hold an answer containing a comma. Decision 2 accepted that for
frontend simplicity, but `inputs_for` with `sort_param` and `drop_param` now
makes a dynamic answer list cheap, so that reason no longer holds.

Votes keep referencing answers by their text, not by the embed id. Answers are
frozen once a ballot is published, so the text is stable and existing votes
need no migration. `Ballot.answer_values/1` reads the embed and replaces
`possible_answers_as_list/1`; nothing ever rebuilds a comma string, because
answers may now contain commas.

## Rejected Alternatives

* `{:array, :string}` -- `inputs_for` raises on array fields, and `cast`
  silently drops blank elements.
* `has_many` -- a new table, a position column, and preloads, with no benefit
  while answers cannot change after publish.

Context: [#200](https://github.com/zorn/flick/issues/200), research in
[#198](https://github.com/zorn/flick/issues/198) and
[#199](https://github.com/zorn/flick/issues/199).
