Style sheet

Six open questions from the round-1 review, each answered in context instead of as swatches. Every sample uses real ballot copy at about phone width, so judge legibility and feel side by side.

Reading the numbers

Ink on strongest mark is the worst case: a letter that lands right on the texture's most visible mark. Every option stays well above 4.5:1, so this choice is about noise and feel, not legibility.

Mark vs ground is how loud a single mark is. 1.00 would be invisible.

Coverage is how much ground the marks take up. Less coverage leaves more clean ground between letters.

02

Contrast on tan

Dark text on tan already measures 15:1, so the unease is perceptual: warmth, a muddier ground, texture behind words. APCA's Lc models perceived contrast better than the WCAG ratio. For body text it asks for Lc 75 at minimum and Lc 90 preferred. These tiles stay in light mode and keep the dot grid.
