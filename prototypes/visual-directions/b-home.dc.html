Don't pick just one. Rank 'em.

Most votes ask everyone for a single choice. In a ranked vote, each voter ranks their favorites instead, and the group lands on a better consensus.

RankedVote.app helps you create, run, and tally a ranked vote, fast. It's free and open source, and nobody needs an account to make a ballot or to vote.

What's on your ballot?

Any choice a group has to make together. A few to get you started:

  • Pick the book club's next book
  • Pick a movie for family night
  • Pick a vacation spot for a big group
  • Pick a new company logo
  • Rank your tech-debt projects, most urgent first

How it works

1, 2, 3. That's it.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Create

    Write a question and the possible answers. That's your ballot.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Publish

    Publish the ballot to get a link. Share the link with your voters.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Tally

    Tally the votes. A point-based count finds the winner.
