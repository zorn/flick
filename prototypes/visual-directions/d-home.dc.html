Rank the choices. Find the consensus.

Most votes ask each voter for one choice. A ranked vote asks voters to put their preferences in order instead, and that leads to a better consensus outcome.

RankedVote.app helps you create, run, and tally ranked-vote ballots quickly. It is free to use and open source. Nobody needs an account to create a ballot or to vote.

How it works

  1. 1

    Step 1: Create

    Write a question and the possible answers.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Publish

    Publish the ballot to get a link, and share it with your voters.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Tally

    Count the votes and find the winner with a point-based count.

Free and open source

The project behind RankedVote.app is called Flick. It is written in Elixir and Phoenix LiveView, and it was built for the Elixir Book Club to pick its books. It is shared for anyone to use and learn from.
