Ask for a ranking, not a single pick.

RankedVote.app helps you create, run, and tally a ranked vote, quickly.

It's free and open source. You don't need an account to create a ballot, and your voters don't need one to vote.

# why rank

One choice each tells you little. An order tells you more.

Most voting asks each voter for a single choice. In a ranked vote, voters rank their preferences instead: a first choice, then a second, and so on.

Counting those rankings finds the answer that more of the group can agree on. That makes for a better consensus than a count of first choices alone.

# how it works

Three steps, start to finish.

  1. 01 |> create_ballot()

    Step 1: Create a ballot

    Write a question and list the possible answers.

  2. 02 |> publish()

    Step 2: Publish it

    Publishing gives you a link. Share it with your voters.

  3. 03 |> tally()

    Step 3: Tally the votes

    A point-based count adds up the rankings and finds the winner.

# open source

Free to use, and free to read.

The project behind RankedVote.app is called Flick. It is written in Elixir and Phoenix LiveView, and it was built for the Elixir Book Club to pick its books.

The code is shared for anyone to use and learn from.
