Instructions to the public

Rank your choices. Find the consensus.

Most votes ask each voter for one choice. A ranked vote asks voters to rank their preferences instead, and that leads to a better consensus outcome.

RankedVote.app helps you quickly create, run, and tally ranked-vote ballots.

How it works

  1. 1

    Create a ballot

    Write a question and its possible answers.

  2. 2

    Publish it

    Publishing gives you a link. Share the link with your voters.

  3. 3

    Tally the votes

    A point-based count finds the winner.

Suitable for

Use a ranked vote whenever a group has to choose together. For example:

  • Picking a book for a book club
  • Choosing a movie for family night
  • Picking a vacation destination for a large group
  • Choosing a new logo for a company
  • Prioritizing technical-debt projects
