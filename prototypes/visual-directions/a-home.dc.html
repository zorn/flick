Ranked voting

Don't just pick one. Rank them.

Rank 1

Most votes ask each person for one choice. A ranked vote asks everyone to put their choices in order, and that leads to a better consensus.

RankedVote.app helps you create, run, and tally a ranked ballot quickly. It's free to use and open source, and no one needs an account to create a ballot or to vote.
