Rank your favorites. Decide together.

Most votes ask everyone for a single choice. A ranked vote asks for their favorites in order, so the result is one more of the group can agree on.

RankedVote.app helps you quickly create, run, and tally a ranked vote. It's free and open source, and nobody needs an account to make a ballot or to vote on one.

How it works

  1. 1

    Create a ballot

    Write your question and list the possible answers.

  2. 2

    Publish and share

    Publishing gives you a link. Send it to your voters.

  3. 3

    Tally the votes

    A point-based count adds up every ranking and finds the winner.

Built for a book club. Shared with everyone.

The project behind RankedVote.app is called Flick. It's written in Elixir and Phoenix LiveView, and it was built for the Elixir Book Club to pick its books. Now it's shared for anyone to use and learn from.
